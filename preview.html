A Note from Jo

hello, friend.

I'm Joanna.

Digital marketing sits somewhere between strategy and aesthetics for me. I like making brands feel considered — whether that means refining a social presence, designing visual content or building a better digital experience.

Most of my work starts with the same question: what should this actually feel like to arrive at? The answer usually changes the plan more than any tool does.

I work quietly and in detail — content calendars, visual systems, page structure, the small edits nobody notices until they are missing.
